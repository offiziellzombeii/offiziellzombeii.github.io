Race Control

You are the Race Director and the Stewards. Choose a race – the simulation drives the cars, you keep the race safe and fair.

Simulation

Fictitious race

Invented teams and drivers on an invented circuit. Everything that happens is simulated: overtakes, collisions, spins, failures, pit stops and rain.

OpenF1 data

Historic race

A real race or qualifying from Formula 1: real circuit, drivers, grid, pace, tyre strategies and rain. The real incidents and retirements happen at the real moment – and your rulings are compared with the real stewards. Older seasons from the data packs (e.g. 2020) are simulated with their cars, drivers and circuits.

Championship

Full season

Run a whole championship as the FIA: up to 24 real circuits, the teams and drivers you choose (from the data packs – built in: 2020 and 2023 onwards), DRS-era or 2026 cars, your own rulebook. The teams build the cars – you run every weekend.

Your job as Race Director

1 · StartSend the cars on the formation lap. When all cars are on the grid, start the light sequence. If a car stalls on the grid: abort the start.
2 · FlagsClick a marshal sector on the map and show yellow / double yellow. A car stopped on the track needs at least double yellow – or a VSC / Safety Car – before the marshals can recover it.
3 · NeutraliseVSC, Safety Car (with "lapped cars may overtake" and "SC in this lap") or a red flag with standing or rolling restart.
4 · StewardNote incidents, investigate, watch the replay, read the evidence and give penalties. Delete lap times for track limits.
5 · ManageEnable DRS 2 laps after the start / a restart, disable it in the rain. Open / close the pit lane. Show black-and-white or black-and-orange flags.
6 · ReportAfter the chequered flag the race report rates your safety response and your decisions.
